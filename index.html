01 / About

Engineering judgement grounded in science.

My background is in chemical engineering, industrial process engineering, research and teaching. This website documents my journey toward developing practical, scientifically defensible Digital Twin systems.

I am a Chemical Engineer with a Ph.D. and experience in industrial process engineering, research, and teaching. My interests include process modelling, multiphysics simulation, environmental technologies, and the development of intelligent industrial systems.Through this research journal, I document my journey in Digital Twin development, including mathematical modelling, computational simulations, experimental investigations, unsuccessful attempts, troubleshooting, and lessons learned.My objective is to bridge fundamental engineering principles with emerging technologies and develop practical solutions for industrial applications.

My present research direction is to bring together first-principles models, industrial measurements, simulation, sensors and computational methods to improve process monitoring and decision-making.

Rather than showing only polished results, this journal will record the technical questions, alternative approaches, simulation errors, experiments, corrections and evidence that shape each project.

A simple research method

Every project begins with a question and concludes with an evidence-based engineering assessment.

  1. Define the question
  2. State assumptions and hypotheses
  3. Build and test the model
  4. Investigate discrepancies
  5. Record limits and next experiments
